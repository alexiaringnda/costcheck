Welcome to CostCheck

The Problem

CostCheck is a community project aimed at addressing the rising cost of living in Bamenda, Cameroon. The project focuses on providing information and resources to help low-income families cope with high rent and food prices.

High rent and food prices in Bamenda have made it difficult for low-income families to make ends meet. Many families struggle to afford basic necessities, leading to increased poverty and social issues.

Who is Affected?

Low-income families in Bamenda are the most affected by the rising living costs. These families often spend a significant portion of their income on rent and food, leaving little for other essential needs.

common challenges

  • High rent prices
  • Expensive food items
  • Lack of affordable housing

what this website can help with

This website can help by providing information and resources to assist low-income families in Bamenda with managing their living costs. We aim to connect them with available support programs and affordable options for housing and food.
